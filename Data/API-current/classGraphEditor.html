◆ DrillIntoGroup()

Renderer * GraphEditor::DrillIntoGroup ( Renderer * hit,
BPoint where )
protected

hit's own Frame() always contains a group's children, so MouseDown()'s top-level hit test alone can never resolve a click to the specific child under the cursor - only ever to the group (issue #38). Recurses for nested groups; returns hit itself unchanged for anything that isn't a GroupRenderer, or if the click landed inside the group's box but not on any of its children.
