◆ Draw()

void GroupRenderer::Draw ( BView * drawOn,
BRect updateRect )
virtual

Renders as a skyline-style boundary that hugs each child's own rect (plus margin) column by column, not one bounding-box rect - a short child stays short even when a taller one sits in a neighbouring column. Gaps with no child at all just hold the last height, keeping the shape one connected piece (issue #38). frame/P_C_NODE_FRAME (RecalcFrame()) stay a plain bounding rect regardless - only Draw()'s own shape changes, hit-testing/serialization are unaffected.
